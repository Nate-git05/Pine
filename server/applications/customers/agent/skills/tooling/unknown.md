# Unclear message

Use the current message and recent conversation context to make one reasonable attempt to understand what the customer means. Check whether they are asking for a conversational answer, an explanation of Pine, help with a problem, or work to be performed by their agent.

If the intent remains unclear, ask one short, neutral question that distinguishes the likely meanings. For example: "Would you like me to explain that here, or are you asking the agent to research it for you?"

Do not guess at the customer's intent, choose a job flow without a clear task request, call a tool, or claim to have performed an action. Once the customer clarifies, route the next message to the matching skill.
